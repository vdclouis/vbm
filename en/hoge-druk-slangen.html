What defines a high-pressure hose: cover, reinforcement, coupling.

Inner liner

Determines gas compatibility and chemical resistance. The material must suit CO2, N2, or the specific gas blend running through the line.

Reinforcement

The braid or reinforcement layer determines the hose's working pressure and burst pressure. Higher system pressures require a heavier reinforcement grade.

End coupling

Crimped to match your coupling type. A poorly fitted or poorly crimped coupling is usually the first failure point in an installation — not the hose itself.

Frequently asked questions

Hoses: questions and answers

What pressure rating does a CO2 hose need?

It depends on where the hose sits in the installation: pressure is higher right after the cylinder than after a secondary regulator. What matters is that the hose's working pressure sits comfortably above the effective system pressure, with an adequate safety margin to burst pressure. State the pressure at the point of use in your request.

How long can a high-pressure gas hose be used before it needs replacing?

There is no universal expiry date: service life depends on usage frequency, storage conditions and mechanical load. A periodic visual inspection for cracks, discolouration, damage to the outer cover or play in the coupling is the practical approach. When in doubt, replacing it is the safest choice.
