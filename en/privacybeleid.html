Personal data we process

Verbeemen D.S. nv processes your personal data because you use our services and/or because you provide this data to us directly. Verbeemen D.S. nv also processes data that customers provide to us in the context of performing an agreement between Verbeemen and the customer.

Overview of the personal data we process on behalf of our customers

  • First and last name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Other personal data you actively provide in correspondence and phone contact
  • Additional data the customer provides to us relating to their activities

Why Verbeemen processes your personal data

  • Processing your payment
  • Contacting you where necessary to fulfil an order
  • Informing you of changes to our services and products
  • Delivering goods and services to you

How long we retain personal data

Verbeemen D.S. nv retains your personal data for the duration of our working relationship and no longer than strictly necessary.

Sharing personal data with third parties

Verbeemen D.S. nv does not sell your data to third parties.

Cookies and similar techniques

Verbeemen D.S. nv does not use cookies. You can refuse cookies by adjusting your browser settings so that no cookies are stored. You can also use these settings to delete previously stored information.

Accessing, changing or deleting data

You have the right to withdraw any consent given for data processing, or to object to the processing of your personal data by Verbeemen D.S. nv, as well as the right to data portability. This means you can submit a request to have the personal data we hold about you sent to you, or to an organisation you designate, in a computer file. You can send a request to access, correct, delete or transfer your personal data, or a request to withdraw your consent or object to processing, to info [at] verbeemen.be.

How we secure your data

Verbeemen D.S. nv takes the protection of your data seriously and takes appropriate measures to prevent misuse, loss, unauthorised access, unwanted disclosure and unauthorised alteration. If you have the impression that your data is not properly secured, or there are indications of misuse, please contact us at info [at] verbeemen.be.

Complaints

You can file a complaint with the Data Protection Authority regarding the processing of personal data by Verbeemen D.S. nv: Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, tel. +32 (0)2 274 48 00, email: commission@privacycommission.be.
